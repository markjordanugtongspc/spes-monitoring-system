Legal Basis: RA 10917 and DO 175

Republic Act No. 10917 amended the SPES law to widen coverage and strengthen support for student beneficiaries. Department Order No. 175, series of 2017, provides implementing rules and guidelines for SPES operations and benefit release.

SPES 2025 Application Guidelines

1 Qualification Standards

  • Age Requirement: 15 to 30 years old at the time of application.
  • Status: Currently enrolled student (Basic Education or ALS) or Out-of-School Youth (OSY).
  • Academic Stand: Passing grades in the most recent academic year/term.
  • Financial Threshold: Combined parental annual income must not exceed the regional poverty threshold.

2 Documentary Requirements

For All Applicants:

  • Updated Resume with 1x1 ID Photo
  • Birth Certificate (PSA issued)
  • Certificate of Indigency/ITR

For Students:

  • Most recent Report Card
  • Certificate of Enrollment (ALS)

3 Application Process

Applications are processed online through the official DOLE Region X portal. Be prepared for an in-person interview if shortlisted.
